How to Connect or Update Your Model

  1. Option A (Instant Sample Model): Run python create_sample_model.py in your terminal. This will automatically export a clean MobileNetV2 architecture to model/model.keras.
  2. Option B (Custom Trained Model): Train your CNN (e.g. ResNet50, EfficientNet, MobileNet) in Jupyter/Colab on the HAM10000 or ISIC dataset and save it as model/model.keras or model/model.h5.
  3. Option C (Change Class Labels): Edit model/classes.json to modify disease names, descriptions, and risk levels.
  4. Click the "Reload Model" button above or restart Flask to activate the changes.
